Uk Birds of Prey

In the UK, we are quite lucky to have a large variety of birds of prey some small and some massive. In this website, I am going to cover different birds of prey, what they do, and how to find them.

What is a bird of prey?

A bird of prey is any bird that hunts and feeds on other vertebrates, mainly mammals, reptiles and smaller birds. Often, they are the top of their food chain, and are the alpha predators, being fast, strong and smart.

What's so amazing about them?

These birds are a pinnacle of evolution and have great eyesight and can detect prey from a distance or during flight, strong feet with sharp talons to kill most prey.
