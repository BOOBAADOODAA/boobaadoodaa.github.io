Harriers

The Marsh Harrier

The western marsh harrier is a large, elegant raptor typically found gliding low over re edbeds and wetlands. It is easily identified by its distinct "V-shape" flight profile, a posture known as a dihedral that helps it remain stable in low-altitude winds. Unlike many other hawks, these birds rel y on a keen sense of hearing to locate small mammals and water birds hidden deep within thick aquatic v egetation.

The marsh harrier is not very common, but they are found around certain mashes in good numbers. RSPB Rainham marshes is brobably the best place to see them.

The Hen Harrier

The hen harrier is a slender bird of prey famous for its "sky dance," a spectacular acrob atic courtship display performed by males over upland moors. This species exhibits extreme sexual dimor phism, with ghostly silver-grey males and larger, streaky brown females often referred to as "ringtails ." Like other harriers, they hunt by flying low over the ground with their wings held in a distinctive, shallow V-shape to surprise small mammals and ground-nesting birds.

Wingspan Lifespan Weight Number of breeding Pairs
97-122cm 7 years 400-600 grams 691

Reserves

Hen harriers hunt red grouse, so they need moorland or upper heathland. Those habitats in The Cairngorms National Park is great as most of the Uk population lives there.

The Montagu's Harrier

The Montagu's harrier is the lightest and most delicate of the harriers, known for its incredibly buoyant and tern-like flight. As a rare summer visitor to the UK, it often nests in agricultu ral cereal fields rather than marshes, which requires close cooperation with farmers to protect the eggs during harvest. Males are distinguished by a ghostly grey plumage with a unique black stripe across th e wing, while the females are brown and streaked.

Reserves

The Montagu's Harrier is arguably the rarest breeding bird in the uk so reserves are hard to guarantee. This is because their breeding sites are kept hidden, and apart from those areas, they are extremely difficult to find in the uk.
